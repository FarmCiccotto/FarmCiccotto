About Us

From our family farm in Racalmuto, we cultivate olive trees using sustainable methods, harvest by hand when possible, and press using cold extraction to preserve aroma and healthy polyphenols.

  • Single-estate — every bottle traceable to our grove
  • First cold press — preserved flavor & nutrients
  • Sustainably farmed — low-impact agriculture

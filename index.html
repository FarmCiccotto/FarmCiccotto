About Us

From our family farm in Italy, Farm Ciccotto cultivates olives with care and presses them using traditional, sustainable methods. Every bottle is traceable back to our grove and crafted to highlight its bright, peppery character.

  • Single-origin — produced on our estate
  • First cold press — for maximum flavor and nutrients
  • Sustainably farmed — minimal intervention, maximum quality

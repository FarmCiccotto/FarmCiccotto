About Us

From our family farm in Racalmuto, we cultivate olive trees using sustainable methods, harvest by hand whenever possible, and cold-extract to preserve aromas and polyphenols.

  • Single-estate — every bottle traceable to our grove
  • First cold press — maximum flavor & nutrients
  • Sustainably farmed — low-impact agriculture
